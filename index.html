Hi, I'm Gosia — and yes, a woman runs this workshop. In this trade that's still rare, and I'm proud of it. I started Mountain Car because I was tired of how garages treat people: the talking-down, the jargon, the bill that somehow beats the quote. Across the industry, studies show 66% of women are charged more than men for the same repair — so I built the opposite.

Here it's one honest price for everyone, man or woman: you get the quote before we touch your car, we show you the old parts, and we explain it all in plain words. No upselling, no surprises. The Members Club is open to all — and the women in Mountain Lejdis get a little extra from me: exclusive discounts at our partners. My personal thank-you to them.

See you soon — Gosia

W całej branży badania pokazują, że 66% kobiet płaci za tę samą naprawę więcej niż mężczyźni — więc zrobiłam odwrotnie.

U nas jest jedna uczciwa cena dla każdego, faceta czy kobiety: wycenę znasz, zanim dotkniemy auta, pokazujemy stare części i tłumaczymy wszystko po ludzku. Bez naciągania, bez niespodzianek.
